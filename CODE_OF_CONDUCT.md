# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior includes:**
- Being respectful and constructive in discussions
- Welcoming contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels
- Gracefully accepting feedback

**Unacceptable behavior includes:**
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer via [GitHub's private contact](https://github.com/adkarp). Reports will be reviewed and responded to promptly.

---

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
